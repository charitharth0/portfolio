THE PROBLEM

One moment of drowsiness
can change everything.

!

Driver fatigue is a serious safety challenge.

Long driving hours, insufficient sleep and fatigue can reduce a driver's attention and reaction time. In many cases, drivers may not realize that they are becoming drowsy until it is too late.

01

Reduced Attention

Fatigue decreases concentration and awareness of the surrounding environment.

02

Slow Reaction

Drowsiness can significantly affect a driver's ability to respond quickly to unexpected situations.

03

Microsleep

Brief involuntary periods of sleep can occur without the driver consciously realizing it.

02

THE SOLUTION

Intelligence that watches
when you can't.

Alert Vision uses computer vision and artificial intelligence to analyze facial behavior and identify visual signs associated with driver drowsiness.

Instead of relying on the driver to manually indicate fatigue, the system continuously observes facial landmarks and calculates indicators such as Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR).

The science behind

01 👁

Eye Aspect Ratio

EAR measures the geometric relationship between points around the eye. A lower value can indicate that the eye is closing.

EAR = (‖p2-p6‖ + ‖p3-p5‖)
───────────────────
2 × ‖p1-p4‖
Detects Prolonged Eye Closure
02 ◯

Mouth Aspect Ratio

MAR measures the opening of the mouth using facial landmark distances and helps identify yawning behavior.

MAR = Mouth Height
