THE PROBLEM

Taking a photo is easy.
Managing it isn't.

◇

Modern image workflows are often fragmented.

Users may need separate applications to capture photos, crop images, store them, organize them and share them. Switching between different tools can make a simple workflow unnecessarily complicated.

01

Multiple Tools

Capture and editing often require separate applications.

02

Manual Management

Organizing and managing captured images can become repetitive.

03

Sharing Friction

Sending images between devices and users can involve unnecessary steps.

02

THE SOLUTION

One flow for your
entire image journey.

CamFlow brings image capture, editing, storage and sharing together inside a single web-based experience.

The platform is designed around a simple principle: reduce the number of steps required to go from capturing an image to sharing the final result.

✓ Capture images directly from the browser
✓ Upload and manage multiple images
✓ Crop images before finalizing
✓ Store and share images easily
